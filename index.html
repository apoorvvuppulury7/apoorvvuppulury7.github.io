Who I Am

I am a final-year undergraduate at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, majoring in Electronics and Communication Engineering (CGPA 8.01/10). My work spans VLSI design, digital IC design, computer architecture, embedded control, and applied research in wireless communication systems and semiconductor device fabrication.

I am drawn to problems at the hardware-software boundary — designing pipelined processors, implementing real-time control loops, modelling stochastic wireless networks, and automating semiconductor characterisation workflows. My goal is to build systems that are efficient, verifiable, and deployable under real engineering constraints.
